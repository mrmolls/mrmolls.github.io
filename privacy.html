Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how MrMolls Uploader (the "App") handles data. The App is a private tool used only by the owner of the Mr. Molls channels to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (@MrMolls) and TikTok account (@mr.molls). It has no other users.

What the App accesses

When the owner signs in with Google or TikTok, the App receives an access token that allows it to upload videos and read basic information about the owner's own channel (channel name, video list, upload status). The App does not access data of any other user, and it does not read comments, messages, contacts or payment information.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload videos and check their status. Video files, titles and descriptions are created by the owner. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

TikTok

For TikTok, the App uses the TikTok Content Posting API only to send the owner's own videos to the owner's own account. TikTok's handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy. Access can be removed in the TikTok app settings.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the App is in use and can be deleted by the owner at any time by removing them from the computer or revoking access as described above.
